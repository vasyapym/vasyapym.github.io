What this does

Groups consecutive letters, numbers, and whitespace. Other symbols become individual pieces. Whitespace receives a dashed outline; numbers use ochre.

What this does not do

It does not implement BPE, SentencePiece, or any model’s vocabulary. Real tokenizers may split a word or merge its leading space. These counts are not model-token or billing estimates.
