tokenfold / timeline

a short history in six marks

1950
turing asks whether machines can think, then swaps the question for a game.
1958
the perceptron learns to tell shapes apart. newspapers over-promise. a pattern begins.
1986
backpropagation makes deep learning trainable. few notice for two decades.
2012
a neural net wins imagenet by a landslide. everyone notices.
2017
"attention is all you need" — the transformer arrives in eleven pages.
2022
conversation becomes the interface. the archive gets a lot longer.
