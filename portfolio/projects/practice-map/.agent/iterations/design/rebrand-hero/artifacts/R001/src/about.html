tokenfold / about

a field guide, not a hype machine

tokenfold is three things sharing one spine: an archive of ideas that built machine intelligence, short lessons that explain them plainly, and small toys you can poke.

we believe understanding beats vibes. every entry is written to be read on paper, in one sitting, without a glossary open in another tab.

the aesthetic is deliberate: ink, grid, monospace. no glowing heads, no lightning bolts. thinking machines deserve calm typography.
