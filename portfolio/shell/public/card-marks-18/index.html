current · split-brain partition (adopted R020)

output 1 · exact 54×16 diagonal lattice, registration-crown leader

output 2 · regular octagon crown, midpoint elbows, bone leader

output 1 — stages snap to an exact diagonal lattice (identical orthogonal elbows); leader becomes a paper block under a thin registration crown; clock ticks + notch lines removed; stage 2 gains a bone cap strip; the bit becomes a crisp square (keeps more chroma as the small focal). risk named: crown louder than the bit — mitigated with 3px ring + 1.5px ticks.

06 · evening forest

current = fox on the trail (green/white pixel fox, teal scene, owner-approved). both outputs refine the older layered-treeline landscape instead.

current · fox on the trail (adopted R016)

output 1 · half-disc moon, stepped-tier bushes, straight ground bar

output 2 · all-polygon understory, faceted tree, layers one rung lighter

output 1 — the blob bushes (the least geometric part of the set) become stepped tiers; back layer → three triangles; curved ground → straight bar; moon cap → exact half-disc with a crisp meridian; fireflies 3→2; canopy clip dropped (plain rect). risk named: tiers reading as buildings — kept low, wide shoulders.

07 · explosion

current · three-frame filmstrip (adopted R018)

output 1 · equal r34→r60 shards, octagonal seam ring, radial embers

output 2 · exact 45° radial system, octagonal lantern

output 1 — shards equalized (all run exactly r34→r60, 10px tips; cardinals bone, diagonals slate); a hairline octagon ring joins the eight radials and seams become short spokes; lantern disc lifts one ramp step; embers become small squares exactly on radials. risk named: octagon reading as a stop sign — 1.2px stroke @ 0.6.

output 2 — one congruent shard shape + one seam, rotated at exact 45° steps (data-driven); embers sit exactly on the 22.5° bisectors; the round lantern becomes a regular octagon with a concentric cap octagon and hairline bone rim; clips removed (overprints become the same polygon in pattern). risk named: perfect symmetry going flat/clip-art — four-tone alternation keeps it alive.
