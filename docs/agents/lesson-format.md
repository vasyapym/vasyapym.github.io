# Lesson format — owner's spec

The idea: each lesson is a way to explore a given topic. I will choose the topics — a variety of them — and this will be done many times in the future.

Format: a non-fiction read — English prose, 800–1500 words. Concept, narrative, style: no limits — that is up to you. I trust your thinking and taste (yours, the writer's — not the orchestrator's).

Audience: lessons should be understandable to beginners and an enjoyable read for people who are tired after work and do not want much cognitive load. At the same time they should not be dull for experts and geeks of the topic.

Two phrases describe what I am after. Treat them as a description of the request, not as direct instructions — they are too vague for that:

1. low-key, engaging humour;
2. low-key PhD-level intelligence and understanding.
