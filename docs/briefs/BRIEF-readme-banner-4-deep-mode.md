# BRIEF 4 — README banner image: an illustration OF the deep mode

Round history: round 1 "too try-hard conceptual"; round 2 name-only typography;
round 3 picked a fox (Evening Forest) — the owner did not recognize why a fox was there.
The lesson: the illustration must connect to a real part of the site, and the chosen part is

**the deep mode (the realm) — the site's hidden dark layer.**

It will sit at the top of two GitHub READMEs (personal profile `github.com/vasyapym` and the
portfolio repo `vasyapym/vasyapym.github.io`, live site <https://vasyapym.github.io>), embedded
as a plain markdown image at full column width. NO text anywhere — the READMEs carry the name
and links; the image must not contain letters, numbers, or word-like marks.

## What the deep mode is (the scene vocabulary you must draw from)

The site has a hidden "deep" layer the visitor can enter ("beneath the surface / enter the
deep"): a dark abyss where a small warm **lantern** of light — the cursor itself — is dragged
through the water, leaving a thin crisp **phosphor trail** (short line chords) and a faint
warm wake. **Seven creatures** swim there, one per project; each is a **door** — on the
threshold below the surface, seven thin door seams stand in the dark, and drifting specks
(snow/dye particles) hang in the water. The abyss deepens downward: surface black `#080f12`,
mid `#04080b`, floor `#020609`. The mood: quiet deep-sea dark, one warm light, everything
else barely there.

## The scene (the illustration must be OF this world)

One quiet moment from that world. Candidate compositions (pick ONE, or one equally true):

- **Lantern descending past the doors**: the surface as ONE thin hairline near the top; below
  it, seven short vertical hairline seams at varying depths (the doors, mostly implied); the
  small warm lantern mid-frame with a single thin phosphor trail curving past two seams.
- **The lantern and one creature**: deep water, the warm lantern light, and ONE barely-lit
  creature silhouette beside it (raft discs / a leaping cat / a lantern-moon shape — the
  creature may hint at a project, but stays dim and abstract).
- **The threshold alone**: the surface hairline with the seven door seams, all neutral ink,
  and the warm lantern light just below — about to enter.
- One more of your choosing, as long as the warm lantern + abyss + doors/creatures vocabulary
  is unmistakable.

## Style frame (deep mode — non-negotiable)

- Background: abyss blacks, deepening downward — `#080f12` → `#04080b` → `#020609`, full-bleed,
  OPAQUE (must look right on GitHub dark and light themes).
- ONE warm light: the lantern in the catalogue's ochre family (`#ffe6c4` core, `#e8b57c` /
  `#d39b61` glow, `#b97f45` deep edge) — the only warmth in the frame; keep it small, ≤~10%.
- Everything else: neutral ink ramp `#26333b #465059 #7d7669 #b6ac95` at LOW weight — hairline
  strokes, dim silhouettes, sparse specks. Mostly empty dark water.
- Restraint: at most one creature hinted (or none); never a crowd; no scene summary.
- The lantern glow is the one permitted soft edge in the whole image (it is literally a light
  in the dark) — no other glows, blooms, neon, or gradients as decoration.

## Hard bans

No text/letters/numbers/word-like marks; no fox, forest, or any other non-deep subject; no
conceptual collages or multi-subject summaries; no neon blooms beyond the lantern itself; no
rainbow hues; no rounded-corner UI frames; no laptops, coffee, robots, code rain, holograms.

## Render constraints

- Master 1280×640 (2:1) — also the exact GitHub social-preview ratio. If the tool only offers
  fixed ratios, take its widest landscape (e.g. 3:2) and keep everything essential inside the
  middle 60% vertical band (2:1-crop-safe).
- Must hold at ~830px wide and smaller: the lantern and doors read at small size; strokes ≥ ~2px.

## Process — full autonomy, shown reasoning

You have FULL design autonomy; no per-decision approvals. Show your reasoning chain explicitly:

1. **Restate** the goal in your own words.
2. Draft **5+ simple** deep-mode scenes (span the candidate list; one moment each).
3. **Prune** with named criteria: deep-mode recognizability, quiet/senior read,
   small-size legibility, 2:1 crop safety.
4. **Develop** the top 1–2: placement and scale of the lantern, door-seam rhythm,
   trail path, how the dark deepens.
5. **Stress-test**: light-theme edge, mobile crop, accidental text/readable symbols,
   lantern too large/bright.
6. **Rank** and commit to ONE.

## Output format (exactly this, nothing else)

1. **Reasoning chain** — six steps, compact.
2. **Committed composition** — 4–6 lines.
3. **Image** — generate the banner at your tool's widest landscape ratio and present it.
4. **Prompt** — the exact, self-contained image-generation prompt you used (plain text,
   paste-ready, all hexes + rules included, no reference to this brief).
