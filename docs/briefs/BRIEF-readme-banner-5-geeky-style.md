**The deep mode (the realm) as a GEEKY, non-photographic illustration.**

## Subject (fixed from round 4 — it was right)

The site's hidden "deep" layer: a dark abyss where a small warm **lantern** of light is
dragged through the water leaving a thin crisp **phosphor trail**, past **seven thin door
seams** (the threshold beneath the surface), with sparse drifting specks. The abyss deepens
downward: surface black `#080f12`, mid `#04080b`, floor `#020609`. One warm light in the
dark; everything else barely there.

## Style (the fix — pick ONE geeky rendering, this is the whole point)

Photographic depth, painterly brushwork, cinematic glow and water/realistic light falloff
are BANNED. The image must look DRAWN BY A PROGRAMMER, in one of these rendering modes:

- **ASCII / monospace raster**: the scene built from a field of abstract monospace glyphs
  (dots, pipes, hashes, math punctuation) — the lantern is the densest/brightest cluster,
  the trail is a thin run of brighter glyphs, the doors are short vertical glyph columns,
  density falls off with depth. Reads as terminal art.
- **Ordered Bayer-dither pixels**: a low-resolution pixel grid quantized with visible dither
  patterns (like 1-bit/retro game postprocessing) — the lantern as a small stepped warm
  cluster, the trail as a dithered dotted line, the doors as sparse darker pixel columns.
- **Flat vector schematic**: crisp hairline geometry on the abyss ramp — lantern as a simple
  stepped circle, trail as a dotted path, doors as vertical hairline segments, specks as tiny
  dots. Blueprint-like, no labels, no annotations.
- One more of your choosing, as long as it reads as terminal/pixel/schematic art, not a
  picture of water.

## Style frame (non-negotiable)

- Background: abyss blacks deepening downward `#080f12` → `#04080b` → `#020609`, full-bleed,
  OPAQUE (must look right on GitHub dark and light themes).
- ONE warm light: the lantern in the catalogue's ochre family (`#ffe6c4` core, `#e8b57c` /
  `#d39b61` glow, `#b97f45` deep edge) — the only warmth; small, ≤~10% of the frame.
- Everything else: neutral ink ramp `#26333b #465059 #7d7669 #b6ac95` at low weight; generous
  empty dark around the subject; subject off-centre.
- Crisp, programmatic rendering: visible grid/dither/glyph structure is a feature, not noise.

## Hard bans

No photorealism, no painterly/watercolor/oil looks, no cinematic lighting or volumetric glow
beyond the lantern itself, no 3D render sheen, no gradient decoration, no readable text/
letters/numbers/word-like marks, no fox/forest/other non-deep subjects, no rainbow hues, no
laptops/coffee/robots/code-rain clichés.

## Render constraints

- Master 1280×640 (2:1) — also the exact GitHub social-preview ratio. If the tool only offers
  fixed ratios, take its widest landscape (e.g. 3:2) and keep everything essential inside the
  middle 60% vertical band (2:1-crop-safe).
- Must hold at ~830px wide and smaller: the lantern and doors read at small size; the dither/
  glyph scale stays coarse enough to stay legible (no sub-pixel noise mush).

## Process — full autonomy, shown reasoning

You have FULL design autonomy; no per-decision approvals. Show your reasoning chain explicitly:

1. **Restate** the goal in your own words.
2. Draft **5+ scenes × rendering-mode** pairs (span the modes above; one moment each).
3. **Prune** with named criteria: geeky-ness (terminal/pixel/schematic read), deep-mode
   recognizability, small-size legibility, 2:1 crop safety.
4. **Develop** the top 1–2: glyph/dither scale, lantern placement and size, trail path,
   door-seam rhythm, how the dark deepens.
5. **Stress-test**: light-theme edge, mobile crop, accidental text, mush at small size.
6. **Rank** and commit to ONE.

## Output format (exactly this, nothing else)

1. **Reasoning chain** — six steps, compact.
2. **Committed composition** — 4–6 lines.
3. **Image** — generate the banner at your tool's widest landscape ratio and present it.
4. **Prompt** — the exact, self-contained image-generation prompt you used (plain text,
   paste-ready, all hexes + rules included, no reference to this brief).
