# BRIEF 3 — README banner image: text-free illustration tied to the portfolio

Round history: round 1 was rejected as "too try-hard conceptual" (dense concept art);
round 2 was rejected as name-only typography. Round 3 is the middle path:

**A small, quiet illustration — no text anywhere — drawn from the portfolio's world.**

It will sit at the top of two GitHub READMEs (personal profile `github.com/vasyapym` and the
portfolio repo `vasyapym/vasyapym.github.io`, live site <https://vasyapym.github.io>), embedded
as a plain markdown image at full column width. The READMEs already carry the name and links —
the banner must NOT contain any letters, words, or glyphs that read as text.

## The connection to the portfolio (this is the brief's core)

The portfolio is six interactive systems. An illustration qualifies if a viewer who opens the
site afterwards would recognize the scene. Candidate subjects (pick ONE, don't summarize all):

- **Raft Cluster** — a few matte discs with stepped tops linked by thin lines, one dashed orbit
  ring around the leader, small axis-aligned squares riding the links (coral `#ff6a5f`).
- **Explosion** — a paper-lantern moon caught mid-blast: a handful of elongated shards flying
  outward on exact radial angles around an empty centre (amber `#ffb347`).
- **Planck to Now** — four nested arcs of increasing radius and fading weight, a violet
  `#a98cff` segment marking "now" on the outermost arc, sparse dots as stars.
- **Evening Forest** — a flat dusk treeline, one seated amber fox silhouette, two firefly dots
  (`#ffb45e` on dusk purples and deep ink).
- **Practice Map** — one thin route line across the plate with small node stops and a single
  sage `#96b896` completion marker.
- **Cat Runner** — a minimal geometric leaping-cat silhouette mid-dash, bone-white `#ece5d8`
  with one ember `#e8863c` accent.

## Style frame (ink catalogue — non-negotiable)

- Background: deep ink `#0b1317`, full-bleed, OPAQUE (must look right on GitHub dark and light).
- Flat vector / screen-print spot-colour look: crisp shapes, thin hairline strokes
  (`#465059` / `#7d7669` / `#b6ac95`), paper `#eeeae0` sparingly for highlights.
- ONE identity hue per illustration, ≤~15% of the frame, as the small focal fill — this mirrors
  the site's six project cards ("Spot-Colour Overprint") and is the tie to the portfolio.
- Generous empty ink around the subject; subject off-centre; the eye lands on one thing.
- Crisp geometry, clean edges, no texture fill over the whole frame.

## Hard bans

No text, letters, numbers, or word-like marks of any kind; no conceptual collages or
multi-subject summaries; no dense generative/noise fields as the main subject; no glow/neon
blooms; no gradients as decoration; no rainbow; no rounded-corner UI frames; no laptops,
coffee, robots, code rain, holograms.

## Render constraints

- Master 1280×640 (2:1) — also the exact GitHub social-preview ratio. If the tool only offers
  fixed ratios, take its widest landscape (e.g. 3:2) and keep the subject inside the middle 60%
  vertical band (2:1-crop-safe).
- Must hold at ~830px wide and smaller: shapes bold enough to read small, strokes ≥ ~2px.

## Process — full autonomy, shown reasoning

You have FULL design autonomy; no per-decision approvals. Show your reasoning chain explicitly:

1. **Restate** the goal in your own words.
2. Draft **5+ simple** directions, each a DIFFERENT single subject from the candidate list
   (or one equally tied subject of your own) — one moment per scene, never a summary.
3. **Prune** with named criteria: portfolio connection strength, quiet/senior read,
   small-size legibility, 2:1 crop safety.
4. **Develop** the top 1–2: placement, scale of the subject vs. empty ink, exact spot hue usage.
5. **Stress-test**: light-theme edge, mobile crop, accidental text/readable symbols.
6. **Rank** and commit to ONE.

## Output format (exactly this, nothing else)

1. **Reasoning chain** — six steps, compact.
2. **Committed composition** — 4–6 lines.
3. **Image** — generate the banner at your tool's widest landscape ratio and present it.
4. **Prompt** — the exact, self-contained image-generation prompt you used (plain text,
   paste-ready, all hexes + rules included, no reference to this brief).
